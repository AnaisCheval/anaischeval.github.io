I am a PhD student in aerospace engineering at the University of Colorado Boulder, in the Autonomous Vehicle Systems (AVS) Lab of Professor Hanspeter Schaub. My research makes Earth-observing satellites decide on board what to image next. I design transformer-based reinforcement learning policies that schedule high-resolution strip imaging, respect battery and reaction-wheel limits, sweep wide areas around clouds, and coordinate a constellation of detection and imaging satellites. The policies are trained and evaluated in the Basilisk spacecraft simulator and BSK-RL, to which I contribute guidance and control modules for strip imaging.

Before my PhD, I obtained a double master's degree in aerospace engineering from KTH Royal Institute of Technology (Sweden) and in engineering from Centrale (France). I wrote my master's thesis on an Erasmus exchange at TU Delft (Netherlands), on robust neural model predictive control of spacecraft orbiting asteroids. I also did two software internships at Airbus Helicopters, in the guidance, navigation and control and the flight control systems departments.
