I am a PhD student in aerospace engineering at the University of Colorado Boulder, in the Autonomous Vehicle Systems (AVS) Lab of Professor Hanspeter Schaub. My research focuses on enabling autonomous and safe continuous Earth observation through transformer-based reinforcement learning architectures for the tasking of satellites and constellations. These policies are trained and evaluated in the Basilisk and BSK-RL simulation frameworks, to which I also contribute.

Before my PhD, I earned a double master's degree from KTH (Sweden) and Centrale (France), wrote my master's thesis at TU Delft on neural predictive control around asteroids, and interned at Airbus Helicopters.
